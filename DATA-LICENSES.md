data under `warmap/data/` comes from other people and keeps its own terms.

## `flock_cameras.json`

Locations and tags of ALPR cameras, compiled by the
[DeFlock](https://deflock.org) project from OpenStreetMap and downloaded from
DeFlock's bulk export (`data.dontgetflocked.com`). The file is a derivative
database of OpenStreetMap data and is made available under the
[Open Database License 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).

Camera locations are copyright OpenStreetMap contributors. The map shows that
attribution on every camera popup and the file carries it in its header. If
you redistribute the file, or a database you built from it, the ODbL applies
to that too. Refresh it with `warmap flock --refresh`.

## `oui.json`

The IEEE MA-L (OUI) registry, pulled from the IEEE Registration Authority's
public listing at `standards-oui.ieee.org` and reduced to a prefix-to-name
table. The listing is published by the IEEE for identifying assigned
prefixes. Refresh it with `python3 tools/fetch_ids.py`.

## `bluetooth-companies.json` and `bluetooth-uuids.json`

Company identifiers and 16-bit service and member UUIDs from the Bluetooth
SIG's published Assigned Numbers, reduced to lookup tables. The names are the
ones the SIG publishes for the purpose of identifying devices. Refresh them
with `python3 tools/fetch_ids.py`.

## `warmap/sample/`

Invented. Every record in the bundled sample session was generated by
`tools/gen_sample_data.py` on a fixed seed and laid out on a street grid in
Phoenix, Arizona, picked for its right-angle streets, not because anyone drove
it. The MACs, names and keys in it are made up.

## `warmap/web/vendor/`

(BSD-2-Clause), vendored unchanged with their own license headers.
